A one-sentence startup pitch for the make.fun competition "Ridiculous at
First Glance, Real by the Second", written by a small deterministic Python
program. The pitch sounds absurd — crows paid in peanuts to pick up
cigarette butts — until the second look: the labour is nearly free, the
butts sell to recyclers, and cities already pay to clean litter by the
kilo.

## The pitch

> We train wild crows to collect cigarette butts, pay them in peanuts, then sell the butts to recyclers and charge cities by the kilo of litter removed.

(150 characters, one sentence.)

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
